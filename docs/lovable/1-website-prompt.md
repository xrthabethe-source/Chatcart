# Lovable prompt 1: the public website (chatcart.co.za)

Paste everything below the line into a new Lovable project. Fill in the `[square brackets]` first, or let Lovable leave them as visible placeholders and fill them in later. **Have a legal professional review the policy pages before going live.** They are written to match how Chatcart actually works, but they're a starting point, not legal advice.

---

Build a mobile-first marketing and information website for **Chatcart**, a South African platform that lets independent direct-selling associates (people who sell health and wellness products from a brand's range) run their own online shop and take orders on WhatsApp. The site lives at **chatcart.co.za**. The actual app (seller sign-in, shops, checkout) lives separately at **app.chatcart.co.za**, so this site only needs static pages and links. It doesn't need a database, accounts or a backend.

## Who the site is for
1. **Associates** who want to sell online. They mostly use phones and WhatsApp and aren't technical.
2. **Customers** who arrive from an associate's shop link and want to know who Chatcart is, and how delivery, payments and refunds work.
3. **Payment providers (Yoco, PayFast) and Meta (WhatsApp)** reviewing the business. They check for clear business details, contact information and policy pages.

## How Chatcart works (use these facts; don't invent others)
- Each associate gets their own shop link (e.g. app.chatcart.co.za/shop/sandiles-shop) to share on WhatsApp and their status.
- Customers order on the shop page or by chatting on WhatsApp, then choose how to receive the order:
  - **Collect at PEP / PAXI:** pick the nearest PEP store. This is the most popular option.
  - **Courier to my door:** The Courier Guy, or the seller's own delivery.
  - **Same-day delivery:** only where a courier confirms it for that address.
  - **Collect from the seller.**
- **Payment:** by card through **Yoco**, straight into the seller's own Yoco account, or by EFT/cash to the seller. Chatcart never holds customers' money.
- **Updates:** customers get WhatsApp messages when the order is paid, booked, in transit, ready for collection and delivered. They can reply **TRACK** any time.
- **Setup:** an associate receives an invite link and sets up their shop on their phone in about 5 minutes. They choose products from the shared catalogue, set delivery and payments, and get a link to share.
- **Their own number:** associates can later connect their own WhatsApp Business number and keep using the WhatsApp Business app.
- Each shop is an **independent seller**. The contract of sale is between the customer and that seller; Chatcart provides the ordering platform.

## Pages
1. **Home**
   - Hero: "Your shop on WhatsApp. Ready in 5 minutes."
   - Three benefits: customers collect at their nearest PEP; secure card payments straight to you; automatic WhatsApp delivery updates.
   - A simple 3-step "How it works".
   - Two buttons: **Become a seller** (goes to the Sellers page) and **Seller login** (links to `https://app.chatcart.co.za/login`).
2. **How it works:** the customer journey (choose products → choose how to receive → pay → WhatsApp updates) and the seller journey (invite link → 5-minute setup → share your link → ship orders).
3. **For sellers:** benefits, what you need (a phone, WhatsApp, optionally a Yoco account), and a FAQ. Joining is by invitation, so the "Apply" button opens WhatsApp to `https://wa.me/[27XXXXXXXXX]?text=Hi%2C%20I'd%20like%20to%20open%20a%20Chatcart%20shop` and also shows the email [hello@chatcart.co.za].
4. **Pricing:** [describe your fees here, or show "Pricing coming soon — contact us"]. Do not invent prices.
5. **Help / FAQ for customers:**
   - How do I track my order? (reply TRACK on WhatsApp)
   - How does PEP / PAXI collection work? (take your ID and order number to the chosen PEP store)
   - Who do I pay? (the seller, through Yoco or EFT)
   - How do I return something? (see Refunds)
6. **Contact:**
   - Business name, registration number, VAT number (if registered), physical address, email, WhatsApp/phone and business hours.
   - Use these placeholders: [Registered company name], [Company registration number], [VAT number or "Not VAT registered"], [Physical address], [hello@chatcart.co.za], [Phone/WhatsApp number], [Business hours].
7. **Terms and Conditions** (`/terms`). Cover:
   - Chatcart is an ordering platform.
   - Shops are independent sellers who sell their own goods.
   - The sale contract is between the customer and the seller.
   - Prices are in rands (ZAR), including VAT where the seller is VAT registered.
   - Payment goes through Yoco directly to the seller, or by EFT/cash to the seller.
   - Orders are confirmed once paid.
   - The delivery options listed above.
   - Customers' rights under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (7-day cooling-off on online purchases) and the Consumer Protection Act (defective goods).
   - Limitation of Chatcart's liability as a platform.
   - Governing law: South Africa.
   - Contact details.
8. **Privacy Policy** (`/privacy`), in line with POPIA. Cover:
   - **What we collect:** name, cellphone/WhatsApp number, email (optional), delivery address or chosen PEP/PAXI point, and order details.
   - **Why:** to process and deliver orders and send order updates.
   - **Who we share it with:** the seller you buy from; couriers (PAXI/PEP, The Courier Guy) to deliver; Yoco to process card payments (card details go only to Yoco); and Meta/WhatsApp to send messages.
   - **Remembered delivery choices:** only with consent ("remember my delivery choice"). On WhatsApp, customers can reply **FORGET** to delete them.
   - Retention, security measures, and the customer's POPIA rights (access, correction, deletion, objection).
   - Information Officer: [Name, email].
   - How to complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
9. **Refunds and Returns** (`/refunds`). Cover:
   - **Cancelling within 7 days** of receiving goods bought online (ECT Act cooling-off). Goods must be unused and in original packaging, where the law allows.
   - **Defective goods:** return within 6 months for a repair, replacement or refund (Consumer Protection Act).
   - **How to start a return:** message the seller on WhatsApp, or contact Chatcart.
   - **Returning a parcel:** usually through PAXI at the customer's cost, unless the goods are defective.
   - **Refunds:** to the original payment method within 30 days of the return being accepted.
   - Health products that have been opened may not be returnable unless defective.
10. **Delivery Policy** (`/delivery`). Cover:
    - Each delivery option and how it works.
    - Delivery times shown at checkout come from the courier or seller and are estimates. PEP / PAXI typically takes several working days; same-day is offered only when confirmed.
    - PEP / PAXI collection needs ID and the order number. Parcels not collected in time are returned to the seller: [add PAXI's current collection period].
    - Tracking by replying TRACK on WhatsApp.
    - Delivery costs are shown before payment.
    - Risk passes to the customer on delivery or collection.

## Footer (every page)
- The business details from the Contact page: name, registration number, address, email and phone. South Africa's ECT Act section 43 requires these for online sellers.
- Links to Terms, Privacy, Refunds, Delivery and Contact.
- "© [year] [Registered company name]".

## Design
- **Mobile first:** most visitors come from WhatsApp on a phone. Use large tap targets and short paragraphs.
- **Mood:** warm, trustworthy, local and South African, not corporate.
- **Colours:** primary green **#0f7b5f** (this matches the app), PEP/PAXI accent red **#c8102e** used sparingly, and a warm off-white background **#f7f6f3**. Support dark mode.
- **Imagery:** simple illustrations of a phone with a WhatsApp chat, a PEP-style parcel counter, and a delivery bike. Do not use the PEP, PAXI, Yoco, WhatsApp or any product brand logos. Refer to them in text only.
- **Language:** plain, friendly English that a first-time online seller understands, with no jargon.
- **Pages:** fast, static, and accessible (good contrast, alt text, semantic headings).
- Do **not** claim any partnership or endorsement by PEP, PAXI, The Courier Guy, Yoco, Meta or any product brand.
- Do **not** add sign-up forms, logins or a database. Sign-in is handled by `https://app.chatcart.co.za/login`.
